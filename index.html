Pricing

What goes into your moving price?

No guesswork and no lowball quotes. We price what you actually move:

Volume of your items

Real cubic feet of your furniture, appliances and boxes – not just the size of your home.

Distance

Miles from your pickup to your drop-off address.

Truck size

A cargo van for small moves, a 26 ft truck for bigger ones – picked automatically.

Stairs & elevators

Walk-ups take more time and muscle – counted separately for both addresses.

Packing

Pack yourself and save, or let us pack – labor and materials are priced per box.

Assembly & specialty items

Assembly per piece; TVs, mirrors, pianos, pool tables and safes get special handling.

FAQ

Questions? We have answers.

When do I see my price?
As soon as you send your name and phone number through the quote tool, your full itemized estimate appears on screen. We receive the same request instantly.

Is the online price final?
It is an estimate based on your inventory, distance, floors and services. We confirm the final price in a short call before your moving day.

What if my building has no elevator?
Pick your floor and choose "no" for the elevator. Walk-ups are priced as a percentage per floor, separately for pickup and drop-off.

Do you take apart and reassemble furniture?
Yes – beds, desks, wardrobes, sectionals and light fixtures. Just tick assembly or disassembly next to each item.

Do you move pianos, pool tables and gun safes?
Yes, with the right equipment. Specialty items have their own price plus a per-flight charge, added automatically.

Can you pack for us?
Yes. Choose "We pack" and the box type – packing labor and materials are added per box.

Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Your belongings are covered from the moment we load them until they are placed in your new home.

Do you move out of state?
Yes. Enter both addresses and the miles – the quote tool includes the distance automatically.
